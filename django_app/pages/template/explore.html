Explore Nepal.

Once is Not Enough

Discover the Land of the Himalayas

Nepal is a land blessed with extraordinary natural beauty and centuries-old cultural heritage — from majestic mountain summits to serene lakes and vibrant traditions.

Culture

Diversity in Culture & Heritage

Nepalese culture is a vibrant tapestry of Hinduism, Buddhism, and rich ethnic traditions — celebrated through year-round festivals, sacred stupas, ancient artwork, and warm hospitality.

Nature

Rich in Natural Wonders

Home to 8 of the world's 10 highest peaks, Nepal draws nature lovers to its pristine alpine lakes, lush jungles in Chitwan, and dramatic mountain landscapes.

Sports & Adventure

Daring Adventures to Experience

For thrill-seekers: high-altitude trekking, white-water rafting, bungee jumping over deep gorges, paragliding over Phewa Lake, and jungle safari expeditions.
